eBay Re-Authorization

Your current token is missing the sell.fulfillment and sell.finances scopes needed for the sales sync. Follow these steps to get a new refresh token.

⚠ Do this once and keep the resulting refresh token safe — it lasts 18 months.
1
Register the callback URL in eBay Developer Portal
Go to developer.ebay.com → User Tokens, find your app, and add this as an Accepted redirect_uri. Copy the RuName eBay assigns to it.
https://ygoexclusives.netlify.app/.netlify/functions/ebay-auth-callback
2
Add EBAY_RU_NAME to Netlify env vars
Netlify → Site Settings → Environment Variables → add EBAY_RU_NAME = the RuName from step 1. Then redeploy once.
3
Enter your RuName and authorize
Paste the RuName here, then click Authorize. You'll be sent to eBay's consent screen.

After authorizing, eBay will redirect back here automatically and display your new refresh token.
